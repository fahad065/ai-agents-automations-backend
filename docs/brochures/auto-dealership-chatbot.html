1. What the Auto Dealership Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your dealership, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. Most buyers research at night and message several dealers at once, while your sales team is busy with customers in the showroom; the bot answers instantly, day or night, from information you control — the cars you have in stock, prices, mileage, specs, finance terms, warranty, trade-ins, servicing and your showrooms. It shares your test-drive booking link or takes a test-drive request, collects trade-in details for an appraisal, and sends every buyer's name and number to your inbox with a one-tap WhatsApp button so a consultant can call while they're still interested. It can show photos of each car and its spec sheet or inspection report as a PDF. It only answers from what you add, so it never invents a price, a car or a finance approval. It works for new and used car dealers, motorcycle dealers and leasing companies in any country, and replies in whatever language each buyer writes in.

What it handles for you

  • Cars in stock — year, trim, mileage, colour, engine, features and price — answered only from your own list
  • Your test-drive link, or a test-drive request with name, phone, car and preferred day and time
  • Finance options, rates, terms and documents, warranty and reservation deposits — examples always "subject to approval"
  • Trade-in enquiries: make, model, year, mileage and photos passed to your team for a valuation
  • Photos of each car and its spec sheet or inspection report PDF, shown under the reply
  • Service prices and service requests for your workshop
  • Every buyer's contact details sent to your inbox
  • All your showrooms and the brands and services each one offers
  • Replies in the language each buyer writes in, including the hand-off message
  • Hand-off to your sales team for offers, trade-in values, finance decisions and complaints
  • Full conversation history, plus WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan
Not on Basic or Pro: live stock from your dealer management system or website feed, test-drive or service booking into your calendar, finance approval or credit checks, instant trade-in valuation, price negotiation, deposits or payments, and CRM integration. These are the dealership extras we build for you on the Custom plan. On Basic and Pro, buyers book through your own link or your team confirms each request — and when a car is sold, you remove its entry and the bot stops offering it straight away.

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your cars, photos, PDFs, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most dealerships that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your booking page, if you use one for test drives or servicing — the bot shares its link; its own fees are set by that provider.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • The bot never asks buyers for card details, ID numbers or bank statements in the chat.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or buyer conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom plan — dealership extras built for you

  • Live inventory sync from your dealer management system or website feed
  • Finance calculator and pre-approval; instant trade-in valuation; deposits and payments
  • CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho…); test-drive and service booking into your calendars
  • Several showrooms or brands as separate bots on one account, dedicated onboarding
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
