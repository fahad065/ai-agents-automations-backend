1. What the Clinic Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your clinic, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. It answers patients instantly, day or night, from a knowledge base you control — services, specialities, doctors, prices, insurance accepted, opening hours, branches and visit instructions. When a patient wants an appointment or to speak to someone, it collects their name and phone number and alerts your reception by email, with a one-tap WhatsApp button so your team can call back while the patient is still choosing a clinic. It only answers from what you add, so it never gives medical advice — medical questions go to your staff. It works for clinics in any country: each branch keeps its own hours, insurance plans and local emergency number, and the bot replies in whatever language the patient writes in. It can show photos of your clinic and team and your price list or visit instructions as a PDF.

What it handles for you

  • Service, doctor and price questions — answered only from your own information
  • Appointment requests — shares your booking link, or takes name, phone, service or doctor and preferred day and time
  • Insurance questions — from the plans each branch accepts, private insurers or public schemes such as Medicare
  • Every patient's contact details sent to your inbox
  • Hand-off to your team for anything medical, a complaint, or a request for a person — it never makes up answers
  • Photos of your clinic, rooms and team, and your price list or visit instructions PDF, shown under the reply
  • Visit preparation instructions — fasting, what to bring, parking
  • All your branches, in one country or several — address, hours, phone and specialities
  • Replies in the language each patient writes in
  • Full conversation history in your dashboard
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan
What it doesn't do: it doesn't diagnose or give medical advice, doesn't see your live appointment calendar (your team confirms each request, or patients book through your own link), doesn't send reminders yet, and never takes payments.

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your knowledge base, photos, PDFs, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most clinics that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or customer conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom pricing

  • Connections to your own systems (your API, clinic management or EMR software)
  • Anything outside the standard plans
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
