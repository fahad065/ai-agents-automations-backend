1. What the Gym Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your gym, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. It answers visitors instantly, day or night, from a knowledge base you control — memberships, prices, joining fees, class timetable, opening hours, branches and facilities. When a visitor wants a free trial, a membership or to speak to someone, it collects their name and phone number and alerts your team by email, with a one-tap WhatsApp button so your sales team can follow up while they're still interested. It can show photos of your gym, equipment and classes and your timetable or price list as a PDF.

What it handles for you

  • Membership and price questions — answered only from your own information
  • Free trial requests — shares your booking link, or takes name, phone and preferred day and time
  • Every lead's contact details sent to your inbox
  • Hand-off to your team when a visitor asks for a person or the bot can't help — it never makes up answers
  • Photos of your gym floor, studios and facilities, and your timetable or price list PDF, shown under the reply
  • Class list, timetable, coaches and facilities
  • All your branches — address, hours, phone and facilities
  • Replies in the language each visitor writes in
  • Full conversation history in your dashboard
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your knowledge base, photos, PDFs, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most gyms that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or customer conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom pricing

  • Connections to your own systems (your API, CRM or membership software)
  • Anything outside the standard plans
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
