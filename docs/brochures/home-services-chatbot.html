1. What the Home Services Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your home-services business, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. Your technicians can't answer the phone up a ladder or under a sink, and customers who don't get an answer call the next company; the bot answers instantly, day or night, from information you control — your services, prices, call-out fees, areas covered, emergency details, maintenance contracts and guarantees. It shares your booking link or takes a job request with the address, the problem and photos, and sends every customer's name and number to your inbox with a one-tap WhatsApp button so your office can confirm the slot. For emergencies it gives your emergency number and after-hours fee straight away. It can show photos of your work and your price list as a PDF. It only answers from what you add, so it never invents a price or promises a technician it can't send. It works for cleaning, AC and heating, plumbing, electrical, pest control, handyman and facility-maintenance companies in any country, and replies in whatever language each customer writes in.

What it handles for you

  • Services, prices, call-out fees, minimum hours and what's included — answered only from your own price list
  • "Do you cover my area?" — answered from the areas, districts or postcodes you add for each branch
  • Your booking link, or a job request with name, phone, address, the problem, photos and a preferred time
  • Emergencies: your emergency number and after-hours fees, straight away
  • Maintenance contracts, guarantees, cancellations and payment methods
  • Photos of your work and your price list PDF, shown under the reply
  • Every customer's contact details sent to your inbox
  • All your branches and the services each one offers
  • Replies in the language each customer writes in, including the hand-off message
  • Hand-off to your team for bigger-job quotes, complaints and business accounts
  • Full conversation history, plus WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan
Not on Basic or Pro: live technician availability and booking into your job-management software (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro…), instant quotes from photos, online payment and deposits, technician tracking and arrival messages, and maintenance-contract renewals. These are the home-services extras we build for you on the Custom plan. On Basic and Pro, customers book through your own link or your team confirms each request.

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your services, photos, PDFs, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most home-services businesses that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your booking page, if you use one — the bot shares its link; its own fees are set by that provider.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • The bot never asks customers for card details in the chat, and never tells anyone how to do a dangerous repair themselves.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or customer conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom plan — home-services extras built for you

  • Live technician availability and booking into your job-management software
  • Instant quotes from photos; online payment and deposits
  • Technician tracking and arrival messages; maintenance-contract renewals and reminders
  • Several brands as separate bots on one account, dedicated onboarding
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
