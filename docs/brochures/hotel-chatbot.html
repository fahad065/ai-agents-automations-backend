1. What the Hotel Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your hotel, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. Guests plan trips at midnight from other time zones, and your front desk is busy with the guests in front of them; the bot answers instantly, day or night, from information you control — your rooms, starting rates, what's included, check-in and check-out, policies, facilities, dining and your own local tips. It shares your booking-engine link so guests book direct, or takes a booking request with dates and number of guests, and sends every guest's name and number to your inbox with a one-tap WhatsApp button so reservations can confirm. It can show photos of your rooms and facilities and your fact sheet or menus as a PDF. It only answers from what you add, so it never invents a rate, a room or a promise. It works for hotels, resorts, serviced apartments, boutique stays and guesthouses in any country, and replies in whatever language each guest writes in.

What it handles for you

  • Room types, sizes, views, starting rates and what's included — answered only from your own information
  • Your booking link, or a booking request with name, phone, email, dates, guests and room type
  • Check-in and check-out, early/late requests, deposits, cancellation, children, pets, parking and transfers
  • Pool, gym, spa, breakfast and restaurant hours, and local tips from your own list
  • Photos of your rooms, pool and views, and your fact sheet or menu PDF, shown under the reply
  • Every guest's contact details sent to your inbox
  • All your properties and the facilities each one has
  • Replies in the language each guest writes in, including the hand-off message
  • Hand-off to your team for booking changes, complaints, special requests, groups and events
  • Full conversation history in your dashboard
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan
Not on Basic or Pro: live availability and exact rates from your PMS or booking engine, booking, changing or cancelling reservations in the chat, payments or deposits, pre-arrival and in-stay messaging, and housekeeping or room-service requests sent into your systems. These are the hotel extras we build for you on the Custom plan. On Basic and Pro guests book through your own link, or your team confirms each request.

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your rooms, photos, PDFs, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most hotels that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your booking engine, if you use one — the bot shares its link; its own fees are set by that provider.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • The bot never asks guests for card details or passport numbers.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or guest conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom plan — hotel extras built for you

  • Live availability and rates from your PMS or booking engine; booking and payment inside the chat
  • Pre-arrival and in-stay messaging; housekeeping and room-service requests into your systems; upsells such as upgrades and transfers
  • Several properties on one account, dedicated onboarding
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
