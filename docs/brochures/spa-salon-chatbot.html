1. What the Spa & Salon Chatbot Does

A chat assistant for your salon or spa, added to your website with one copy-paste line of code. Your team can't answer the phone in the middle of a colour or a massage; the bot answers clients instantly, day or night, from information you control — your treatment menu, prices, durations, packages, gift cards, opening hours and branches. It shares your online booking link (Fresha, Booksy, Vagaro, Treatwell or your own system) or takes an appointment request, and sends every client's name and number to your inbox with a one-tap WhatsApp button so your front desk can confirm. It can show photos of your treatments and rooms and your price menu as a PDF. It only answers from what you add, so it never invents a price or gives skin or medical advice. It works for salons, spas, barbershops, nail, lash and beauty studios in any country, and replies in whatever language each client writes in.

What it handles for you

  • Treatment, price and duration questions — answered only from your own menu
  • Your online booking link, or an appointment request with name, phone, treatment and preferred day and time
  • Packages, memberships, bridal and group bookings, gift cards, deposits and cancellation policy
  • Photos of your treatments, rooms and work, and your price menu PDF, shown under the reply
  • Every client's contact details sent to your inbox
  • All your branches and the services each one offers
  • Replies in the language each client writes in, including the hand-off message
  • Hand-off to your team for allergies, pregnancy, skin reactions and complaints — it never gives medical advice
  • Full conversation history in your dashboard
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and analytics on the Pro plan
Not on Basic or Pro: live booking into your booking system, your team's live availability, appointment reminders, no-show follow-ups, payments or deposits, and loyalty. These are the salon extras we build for you on the Custom plan. On Basic and Pro your team confirms each request, or clients book through your own link.

3. Full Cost Breakdown — What's Covered, What Isn't

We want the full cost to be clear before you commit. Beyond the plan price above:

Covered by your LogicMate plan

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Storage for your menu, photos, price menu PDF, conversations and account — within your plan's photo and PDF limits
  • Platform maintenance, updates and every feature in your plan

Brought by you

  • Your own AI key — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini. The chatbot runs on your own account, billed directly by your AI provider for what you actually use; for most salons that's a small monthly amount. LogicMate never marks up or resells this, and never holds a shared key for you.
  • Usage and top-ups — check usage and top up any time in your own AI provider account.
  • Your booking system, if you use one (Fresha, Booksy, Vagaro, Treatwell…) — the bot shares its link; its own fees are set by that provider.
  • Your own Meta Business account, only if you enable WhatsApp or Instagram (Pro). You (or our team, with your permission) add its IDs, access token and app secret in the Channels tab. Meta's own fees and policies are set by Meta.

Security

  • Your AI key is stored encrypted (AES-256).
  • WhatsApp and Instagram messages are only accepted when signed by your own Meta app.
  • LogicMate never uses your business content or client conversations to train any AI model, and never sells your data.

Custom plan — salon extras built for you

  • Live booking into your booking system and your team's real availability
  • Appointment reminders, no-show follow-ups, deposits or payments, loyalty
  • Several branches or brands on one account, large spas and hotel spas, dedicated onboarding
  • Scoped and quoted individually once we know your requirement — never billed automatically.
